How It Works施工流程

A smooth renovation comes from a clear process. From the first conversation to the final handover, you'll always know where things stand. 一次顺利的装修，来自清晰的流程。从初次沟通到最终交付，每一步您都清楚正在发生什么。

STEP 01第一步

Consultation & Site Visit咨询与现场了解

We start by listening to what you have in mind, then visit the site to see the actual conditions and discuss what's workable. 我们先听您想要什么，再到现场看房屋的实际情况，一起讨论可行的做法。

STEP 02第二步

Scope & Team Matching方案沟通与团队匹配

We define the scope and budget, then connect you with the independent partner team suited to your project. 明确施工范围与预算，为您对接适合这个项目的独立合作团队。

STEP 03第三步

Coordination During Construction施工对接与进度协调

During construction we're your point of contact — coordinating schedules, following up on materials, and flagging issues early. 施工期间我们是您的对接人，协调进度、跟进材料，遇到问题及时沟通。

STEP 04第四步

Final Check & Handover完工确认与交付

We walk through the finished work with you and make sure nothing is left outstanding before handover. 陪同您检查完工情况，确认没有遗留问题后再正式交付。
